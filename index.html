Ohm's law

          
    
            
  • V = I × R
    • 
            
  • I = V / R
    •

Power

    • 
            
  • P = I² × R
    • 
            
  • P = V² / R

Voltage is the electrical potential difference, current is the flow of electric charge, and resistance opposes current flow.
